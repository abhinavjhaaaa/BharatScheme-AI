CENTRAL SECTOR SCHEME OF SCHOLARSHIP FOR COLLEGE AND UNIVERSITY STUDENTS (PM-USP CSSS)
OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES
MINISTRY OF EDUCATION, DEPARTMENT OF HIGHER EDUCATION
GOVERNMENT OF INDIA

1. OBJECTIVE OF THE SCHEME
The Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students (PM-USP CSSS) is implemented by the Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education, Government of India. The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to meritorious students from low-income families to meet day-to-day expenses while pursuing higher education after senior secondary (Class 12).

2. ALLOCATION AND QUANTUM OF SCHOLARSHIPS
A total of 82,000 new scholarships are awarded every year across India. The scholarships are divided equally between boys (41,000) and girls (41,000). The scholarships are distributed among the State Education Boards based on the population in the age group of 18-25 years in the respective State.

3. RATE OF SCHOLARSHIP AND FINANCIAL BENEFITS
Under the PM-USP CSSS scheme, financial assistance is paid directly to eligible students via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into their Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts:
- At Graduation Level (Undergraduate): Rs. 12,000 per annum for the first three years of degree courses (Years 1, 2, and 3).
- At Post-Graduation Level: Rs. 20,000 per annum for 2 years of postgraduate studies (e.g., M.A., M.Sc., M.Com.).
- For 5-Year Integrated / Professional Courses (like B.Tech, Integrated M.Sc): Rs. 12,000 per annum is paid for the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd year, and Rs. 20,000 per annum is paid for the 4th and 5th year.

4. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
To be eligible for the PM-USP CSSS scholarship, an applicant must satisfy the following conditions:
(a) Merit Requirement: The student must have scored above the 80th percentile of successful candidates in the relevant stream (Science, Commerce, Humanities) from the respective Board of Examination in Class 12 (10+2 pattern).
(b) Enrollment: The student must be enrolled in a regular, full-time degree course in a recognized College, Institution, or University recognized by UGC, AICTE, MCI, or DCI. Correspondence, open university, and distance education courses are strictly ineligible.
(c) Family Income Ceiling: The gross annual family/parental income from all sources must not exceed Rs. 4,50,000 (Rs. 4.5 Lakh) per annum. A valid income certificate issued by a competent revenue authority is mandatory.
(d) Exclusivity: The student must not be receiving any other scholarship, fellowship, or fee concession/reimbursement from the Central Government or State Government.
(e) Reservation: Reservation quotas apply as per Government of India norms (15% for SC, 7.5% for ST, 27% for OBC-NCL, and 5% horizontal reservation for Persons with Disabilities).

5. RENEWAL OF SCHOLARSHIP
Scholarships are renewed on an annual basis up to the completion of the degree:
- The scholar must secure at least 50% marks in the annual university/college examination.
- The scholar must maintain at least 75% attendance in the institution.
- Any change in the course of study or college without prior approval shall lead to forfeiture of scholarship.

6. HOW TO APPLY AND REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
Applications must be submitted online exclusively through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at https://scholarships.gov.in/.
Mandatory documents include:
- Aadhaar Card and Aadhaar-seeded active bank account passbook
- Class 12 Board Examination Marksheet and Passing Certificate
- Income Certificate issued by competent revenue authority (income <= Rs. 4.5 Lakh)
- Bonafide student admission certificate from the enrolled college/university
- Caste / Disability certificate (if applicable)
